Deneme sonucu (20 Kasım): Reading 4.5 · Listening 5 · Writing 2 · Speaking 3.5 → Toplam 4

Hedef: Toplam 5 (dört bölümün toplamı 19 olursa yuvarlama ile 5'e çıkar)

En hızlı puan kazanılacak yerler sırasıyla: Writing (Build a Sentence + Academic Discussion), Speaking (cevap uzunluğu + fiil zamanları), Academic Talk ve Complete the Words.

Her konunun altında "Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse" kutusu var: örnek soru ve o kuralı doğru kullanan örnek cevap.

1. Dilbilgisi: En Sık Yaptığın Hatalar

1.1 Durum fiilleri -ing almaz

prefer, think (görüş), agree, understand, know, like, want, need, believe gibi fiiller düşünce ve his anlatır, eylem değil. Bu yüzden şimdiki zamanda bile -ing almazlar.

Yanlış Doğru
I am preferring quiet places. I prefer quiet places.
I am choosing my hobby alone. I prefer hobbies I can do alone.
I am agree / I am agreeing. I completely agree.
I am understand the point. I understand why some people think…
Reading is developing imagination. Reading develops imagination.

Kural: Konuşmaya "I am…" ile başladıysan ve arkasından prefer/agree/understand geliyorsa dur, "am"i at.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: Do you prefer studying at home or in a library?

Cevap: "I prefer studying at home. I know that libraries are quieter, but at home I feel more comfortable and I don't need to travel. I understand why some students like libraries, though, because there are fewer distractions. So, overall, I focus much better at home, because I can study in my own way."

→ prefer, know, feel, need, understand: hepsi sade, hiçbiri -ing değil. Kapanışta da focus present simple, ve much better(✗ more better).

1.2 Alışkanlık = present simple, eski alışkanlık = used to

Yanlış Doğru
I am working as a software engineer. I work as a software engineer.
We are using AI tools. We use AI tools every day.
I am going to Istanbul each month. I go to Istanbul every month.
I am using them every day. I use them almost every day.
(geçmiş) In Istanbul I spend two hours… In Istanbul I used to spend two hours on the road.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: How has your daily routine changed in recent years?

Cevap: "My routine has changed a lot in recent years, mainly because I moved from Istanbul to Urla. In Istanbul, I used to wake up at six and spend two hours in traffic every day. Now I work from home three days a week, so I get up later and go for a walk by the sea before work. For example, this morning I walked for half an hour and still started work on time. That said, I miss the energy of the city sometimes. So, overall, my routine is much healthier now."

→ Cevap → sebep → eski alışkanlık used to → şimdiki alışkanlık present simple → örnek past simple → karşı görüş → sonuç.

1.3 Geçmiş hikâyede geçmiş zamanda kal

Hikâye anlatırken şimdiki zamana kayıyorsun. "When I was a child", "last year", "ago" duyduğun anda tüm fiiller geçmiş zaman olmalı.

  • ✗ I learn how to cook when I was a child. → ✓ I learned how to cook when I was a child.
  • ✗ It just get three hours. → ✓ It took three hours.

Past simple mı, present perfect mi?

  • ago, yesterday, last week, in 2023, when… → past simple: I moved to Urla two years ago.
  • since, for, yet, already, ever, recently → present perfect: I have lived in Urla for two years.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: Tell me about a new skill you learned recently.

Cevap: "One new skill I learned recently is sewing. The main reason I started was that I wanted a hobby away from screens. For example, last year I watched online videos and made a simple bag. Then I bought a sewing machine and sewed my first dress. Since then, I've made five pieces of clothing. Of course, it wasn't easy at first, and I made lots of mistakes. So, overall, sewing has become my favorite way to relax."

→ Belirli zamandaki olaylar past simple, "since then" ile present perfect.

1.4 Zaman için "take" kullan

It takes + (kişi) + süre + to + fiil

  • ✗ which would normally get six months → ✓ which would normally take six months
  • It takes me ten minutes to get to work.
  • It took us one week to build the system.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: How do you usually get to work?

Cevap: "I usually drive to work. The main reason is that buses in Urla aren't very frequent. It takes me about fifteen minutes to get to the office, and I only go three days a week. For example, in Istanbul it used to take me more than an hour each way, so I often arrived at work tired. Of course, driving isn't great for the environment. So, overall, my commute is much shorter and less stressful now."

1.5 Tekil / çoğul ve a / the

Yanlış Doğru
The biggest advantages is… The biggest advantage is…
big cities is big cities are
practical skills like cooking is practical skills like cooking are
lots of chance many chances
a new opportunities new opportunities (çoğulda "a" yok)
academic subject (genel) academic subjects

a/the: Tekil sayılabilir isim tek başına kalamaz: ✗ I am software engineer → ✓ I am a software engineer. Daha önce bahsedilen ya da tek olan şey için the: the professor, the library, the meeting.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: What is the biggest problem in big cities?

Cevap: "In my opinion, the biggest problem is traffic. The main reason is that big cities have too many cars, and public transport is often crowded. For example, when I lived in Istanbul, it took me two hours a day to get to work and back. People spend many hours on the road, so they have fewer chances to relax. Of course, big cities have many advantages too. But overall, traffic is the biggest problem."

→ Tekil özne + is, çoğul özne + are/have.

1.6 Karşılaştırma

  • ✗ more easier / more happy → ✓ much easier / happier / far more convenient
  • Kısa sıfat: -er (cheaper, faster). Uzun sıfat: more (more expensive). İkisi birlikte asla.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: Is online shopping better than shopping in stores?

Cevap: "I think online shopping is better for most people. The main reason is that it's much easier and usually cheaper. For example, last month I ordered fabric for my sewing projects online, and it was far more convenient than driving to Izmir. However, shopping in stores is more enjoyable, because you can check the quality and try things on. So, overall, online shopping is the better choice for busy people."

1.7 Edat ve küçük kelime hataları

Yanlış Doğru
on next Friday next Friday (next/last/this önünde edat yok)
in these days these days / nowadays
ask to you ask you
in there there
on our daily basis on a daily basis / in daily life
on the screen (uzun süre) in front of a screen
with myself by myself
the chance of learning the chance to learn

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse (e-posta cümlesi)

Görev: Ask your manager to move the meeting.

Cevap: "I would like to ask you whether we could move the meeting to next Friday. These days, I am working on a deadline, and I spend most of the day in front of a screen with the client. I would appreciate the chance to prepareproperly."

1.8 Cümle bağlama

Virgülle iki tam cümle bağlanmaz.

  • ✗ I moved to Urla, it is very quiet.
  • ✓ I moved to Urla, and it is very quiet. / I moved to Urla. It is very quiet. / I moved to Urla, which is very quiet.

Arka arkaya iki "however" kullanma; ikincisini but ya da that said yap. "As a conclusion" yerine So, overall.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru: Should people work from home?

Cevap: "I think hybrid work is the best option. I work from home three days a week, which saves me a lot of time. However, I miss my colleagues sometimes, so I go to the office twice a week. That said, I would never go back to working full time in the office. So, overall, hybrid work gives me the best balance."

1.9 Yazım listesi (sık yanlış yazdıkların)

Could (cloud değil!) · original · Alternatively · conference · healthier · difficult · refreshments · article · before · author · rubbish · ourselves · surprise · invite

Final check sırasında önce sadece could kelimesini ara.

2. Writing

2.1 Build a Sentence (3/7 → hedef 5+)

Adımlar:

  1. Önce soru mu, düz cümle mi bak (soru işareti, "Do you know…", "I wonder…").
  2. Fazla parçayı (tuzak) bul: aynı fiilin iki hali varsa (announce / announced, close / closes) biri fazladır.
  3. Sıra: özne → fiil → nesne → yer → zaman.
  4. Emin olmadığın parçayı en sona bırak.

Soru sırası:

  • Evet/hayır: Yardımcı fiil + özne + fiil → Did you leave your umbrella at the station?
  • Wh- soru: Wh- + yardımcı + özne + fiil → When does the library close?

Dolaylı soru (en çok kaybettiğin yer): Do you know / Can you tell me / I wonder / I'm not sure sonrasında düz cümle sırası gelir, yardımcı fiil öne geçmez.

  • ✗ Do you know when does the library close? → ✓ Do you know when the library closes on Sundays?
  • ✗ Can you tell me where is the meeting room? → ✓ Can you tell me where the meeting room is?
  • ✗ I wonder when will the results be announced. → ✓ I wonder when the results will be announced.
  • Wh- kelimesi yoksa if / whether: I'm not sure if she has finished the report.

Diğer kurallar: İki yardımcı fiil yan yana gelmez (✗ did has). There is/are sorusunda Is there…? (✓ Is there a pharmacy near here?). Sıfat sırası: görüş → boyut → yaş → renk (a nice small old red car).

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Örnek 1

Konuşma: "I missed the first lecture."

Cevap: Do you know ___ ___ ___ ___ ___ ?

Parçalar: covered · the professor · what · today · did · cover

✓ Do you know what the professor covered today?

→ Dolaylı soru, düz sıra. did ve cover tuzak (dolaylı soruda did öne gelmez).

Örnek 2

Konuşma: "The new café opened last week."

Cevap: ___ ___ ___ ___ ___ ?

Parçalar: you · have · been · there · yet · went

✓ Have you been there yet?

→ yet = present perfect. went tuzak.

Örnek 3

Konuşma: "Why are you so late?"

Cevap: ___ ___ ___ ___ ___ ___ .

Parçalar: the bus · because of · was · delayed · the snow · delay

✓ The bus was delayed because of the snow.

→ Edilgen yapı was + V3. delay tuzak.

2.2 Write an Email (4/5, korunacak)

  • 0:00–3:30: Senaryodaki her maddeyi "I" ile yeniden yaz, her madde 2 cümle (~90 kelime). Maddedeki fiili cümlene taşı.
  • 3:30–5:30: Her paragrafa bir cümle ekle: sonuç (As a result…), alternatif (Alternatively…), sayı/tarih.
  • 5:30–7:00: Kontrol: madde atlanmış mı (özellikle "and" ile iki parçalı maddeler), sayılar görevle aynı mı (%30 ≠ %20), could yazımı.
  • Diplomatik dil: olayı özne yap (The late cancellation left us…), It might help to…, I would appreciate it if…, Should this not be possible…

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Görev: You signed up for a weekend cooking class at a community center, but it was cancelled one day before it started. Write an email to the center manager, Ms. Clark. In your email:

  • explain what happened
  • describe how the cancellation affected you
  • suggest a solution

Cevap (~125 kelime):

Dear Ms. Clark,

I am writing about the Italian cooking class that was scheduled for last Saturday. I registered three weeks ago and paid the full fee of $60. However, I received an email only one day before the class, saying that it had been cancelled.

The late cancellation left me in a difficult position. I had already bought ingredients for the first session, and I had turned down another plan with my friends. As a result, I lost both money and my weekend.

It might help to offer a replacement class next month. Alternatively, I would appreciate it if you could refund my fee. Should this not be possible, a discount on a future course would also be acceptable.

Thank you for your understanding.

Best regards,

Sena Oz

→ Üç madde = üç paragraf. Her paragrafta ayrıntı + sonuç. Sayılar ve tarih somut.

2.3 Academic Discussion (3/5 → hedef 4–5)

Denemedeki üç sorun: görüş ilk cümlede yoktu, metin yarım kaldı, örnek fikre bağlanmadı.

Kalıp (120–140 kelime, 10 dakika, ilk taslak 5:00'te biter):

  1. İlk cümle = net cevap: I strongly believe that… / In my view, cities should…
  2. Bir öğrenciye adıyla cevap: I see Ali's point that…, but…
  3. Yeni fikir (iki öğrencinin söylemediği).
  4. Somut örnek + bağlantı: For example, … This shows that…
  5. Kısa sonuç: That is why I believe…

Süre biterken yarım cümle bırakma; 9:00'da ne yazıyorsan bitir ve sonuç cümlesini ekle.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Profesör: Many cities are growing quickly. Some people say governments should encourage people to move to small towns instead. Do you agree? Why or why not?

Maya: Yes. Big cities are overcrowded and expensive, so moving people to small towns would reduce pressure on housing.

Daniel: No. Most jobs and universities are in big cities, so people would lose opportunities.

Cevap (~130 kelime):

I strongly believe that governments should encourage people to move to small towns. I see Daniel's point that most jobs are in big cities, but this is changing quickly. Thanks to remote work, many people no longer need to live near their office. Governments could support this trend by improving internet access and transport in small towns. For example, I moved from Istanbul to a small town two years ago, and I still work for the same company because I can work online. I now spend much less time in traffic and pay lower rent. This shows that people can keep their careers without living in crowded cities. That is why I believe governments should help more people move to smaller towns.

→ İlk cümle net görüş · Daniel'e adıyla cevap · yeni fikir (uzaktan çalışma, ikisi de söylemedi) · kişisel örnek + "This shows that" · sonuç.

3. Speaking

3.1 Interview (3/5 → hedef 4)

Denemedeki sorunlar: cevaplar 40–55 kelime (çok kısa), örnek yok, durum fiillerinde -ing.

45 saniye ≈ 80–90 kelime. Kalıp:

Bölüm Kalıp
Cevap I think… / It depends, but in my case…
Sebep The main reason is that…
Örnek For example, last year I… (geçmiş zaman!)
Karşı görüş Of course… / That said, …
Sonuç (~35. sn) So, overall, …

"For example" sonrası gerçek bir olay gelmeli, sebep değil. ✗ For example, because we don't have… → ✓ For example, I studied physics at school, and I use it every day.

Hikâye bankan: İstanbul → Urla (trafik, aylık İstanbul ziyareti) · hibrit çalışma · AI araçları (6 ay yerine 1 hafta) · Belgrad Ormanı yürüyüşü · dikiş + ekransız hafta · lisede İngilizce öğrenme · yakındaki bağ keşfi.

Band 6 kelimeler: recharge, switch off, pick up (a skill), in depth, essential, meanwhile, prioritise, that said, instead of + -ing.

Takılınca: Let me think… / That's an interesting question… (sessizlik yerine).

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Soru 1: Some people think living in a small town is better than living in a big city. Do you agree?

Cevap: "I agree, mainly because life in a small town is much less stressful. The main reason is traffic. In Istanbul, I used to spend almost two hours on the road every day. Now I live in Urla, and I can get anywhere in ten minutes. So instead of sitting in traffic, I spend time with my family or explore new places. That said, I still go back to Istanbul every month to see friends. So, overall, a small town gives me a better quality of life."

Soru 2: Do AI tools make employees more productive?

Cevap: "I think AI tools definitely make employees more productive. The main reason is that they handle repetitive tasks, so we can focus on harder problems. For example, my team recently built a system in just one week, which would normally take about six months. However, it also creates pressure, because companies now expect us to always befaster. So, overall, AI gives us great opportunities, as long as we keep a healthy work-life balance."

Soru 3: Do you prefer hobbies you do alone or with others?

Cevap: "It depends on my mood, but most of the time, I prefer hobbies I can do alone. The main reason is that I spend all day in meetings and in front of a screen, so I need quiet time to recharge. For example, I started sewing last year, and creating something with my own hands makes me much less stressed. That said, I still join group hikes now and then. So, overall, solo hobbies suit my lifestyle better."

Soru 4: Should schools teach practical skills or academic subjects?

Cevap: "I believe schools should focus on academic subjects like math and science. The main reason is that we rarely get the chance to learn them in detail later in life. For example, I studied math and physics at school, and I use them almost every day as a software engineer. Of course, practical skills like cooking matter too, but I learned those from my family as a child. So, overall, schools should focus on academic subjects."

→ Kalın yerler kalıp: her soruda aynen kullanabilirsin. Sadece aradaki konu kelimelerini değiştir.

3.2 Listen and Repeat (3/5)

Cümleyi kelime kelime değil anlam grupları halinde dinle: The library will close early | on Friday | because of the holiday. Küçük kelimeler düşmesin: a / the, -s, -ed, at / on / in. Uzun cümlede önce yapıyı (özne + fiil) yakala, ayrıntıyı sonra ekle.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

(Bir spor salonu görevlisi yeni üyelere bilgi veriyor. Sesli oku, sonra kitaba bakmadan tekrar et.)

  1. Welcome to the gym.
  2. | Lockers are | on the left. |
  3. | Please bring | your own towel. |
  4. | The pool is closed | on Mondays | for cleaning. |
  5. | If you need help, | ask any of the trainers | at the front desk. |
  6. | Group classes start | at seven a.m. | and you can sign up | online. |

4. Reading ve Listening

4.1 Complete the Words (21/30)

  1. Boşluğun kelime türünü bul: to + fiil, the + isim, isimden önce sıfat, fiilden sonra zarf (-ly).
  2. Harf sayısına uy ve gerçek bir kelime olduğundan emin ol.
  3. Sık ekler: -tion, -ment, -ly, -ive, -ness, -able.
  4. Kısa boşluklar çoğu zaman: the, that, from, with, which, their, been.
  5. Tekil/çoğul ve zaman uyumunu cümleden kontrol et (-s, -ed).

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Bees play an import___ role in agriculture. Many crops depe___ on them for pollin______. When a bee vis___ a flower, it car___ pollen fr__ one plant to anot___. In rec___ years, however, bee popula_____ have decreased dramat______, which has wor____ scientists and farmers.

Cevaplar ve nedeni:

  • important → an + ___ + isim (role): sıfat
  • depend → crops + fiil (çoğul özne, -s yok)
  • pollination → for + isim: -tion
  • visits → a bee tekil: fiil -s alır
  • carries → aynı özne, -s
  • from → from… to kalıbı
  • another → one… another
  • recent → years'tan önce sıfat
  • populations → have decreased: çoğul isim
  • dramatically → fiilden sonra zarf: -ly
  • worried → has + V3

4.2 Academic Talk (6/12)

Not alırken dört şeyi yakala:

  1. Konu (ilk 2–3 cümle).
  2. Tanımlar / iki taraf (X is…, on the other hand).
  3. Örnek ve neden verildiği: Soru genelde "Hoca bu örneği neden verdi?" Cevap çoğunlukla bir kavramı açıklamak için.
  4. Son cümle: Next time we'll… gibi ifadeler "sonra ne olacak" sorusunun cevabıdır.

Sınavda böyle bir soru gelirse

Konuşma (özet): "Today we're talking about the mere exposure effect. This is the idea that people tend to like things more simply because they've seen them many times. For example, think about a song you didn't like the first time you heard it on the radio. After hearing it every day for a week, you probably started humming along. Advertisers use this effect a lot, which is why we see the same ads over and over. Next time, we'll look at when this effect stops working."

Notların: Konu: mere exposure effect · Tanım: tekrar görünce daha çok sevmek · Örnek: radyodaki şarkı · Reklamcılar kullanıyor · Next: etkinin işe yaramadığı durumlar

Soru 1: What is the talk mainly about?

✓ How repeated exposure can make people like something more.

Soru 2: Why does the professor mention a song on the radio?

✓ To illustrate how familiarity can change people's opinions.

→ "Neden bahsetti?" sorusunun cevabı neredeyse her zaman to illustrate / to explain a concept.

Soru 3: What will the professor discuss next?

✓ Situations in which the effect does not work.

→ Son cümle = son soru.

5. Alıştırmalar

A. Doğru fiil hali

  1. I ___ (prefer) quiet places when I study.
  2. In Istanbul, I ___ (spend) two hours in traffic every day. (geçmiş alışkanlık)
  3. Last month, I ___ (sew) a dress for myself.
  4. I ___ (live) in Urla since 2024.
  5. It ___ (take) us one week to build the system last month.

B. Dolaylı soruya çevir 6. Where is the bus stop? → Can you tell me ___? 7. When does the course start? → Do you know ___? 8. Will she come? → I wonder ___.

C. Hatayı düzelt 9. The biggest advantages of small towns is the traffic. 10. I learned it on next Friday, cloud you help me?

Cevap anahtarı

  1. prefer · 2. used to spend · 3. sewed · 4. have lived · 5. took
  2. where the bus stop is · 7. when the course starts · 8. if/whether she will come
  3. The biggest advantage of small towns is the traffic.
  4. I'll learn it next Friday. Could you help me?
