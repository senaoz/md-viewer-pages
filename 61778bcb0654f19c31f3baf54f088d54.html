TOEFL: Son 2 Saat

Bu üç sayfa dışındaki her şeyi zaten biliyorsun. Reading ve Listening'de 4.5–5 aldın, orada güvendesin. Puan Speaking, Email ve Academic Discussion'dan gelecek. Bunları oku, kalıpları sesli söyle, sonra dinlen.

1. Speaking Interview: 5 Durak, 45 Saniye

Her soruya aynı beş durakla cevap ver. Kalıplar sabit, sadece konu kelimeleri değişir.

Durak Saniye Kalıp
1. Cevap 0–5 I think… / I believe… / I agree, mainly because… · Kararsızsan: It depends, but most of the time, I prefer…
2. Sebep 5–12 The main reason is that…
3. Örnek 12–30 For example, last year I… (hikâye bankasından, geçmiş zaman)
4. Karşı görüş 30–37 That said, I still… / Of course, … matters too, but…
5. Sonuç 37–45 So, overall, …

Takılırsan: Let me think… · That's an interesting question… · It depends, but in my case… Bunları söylerken düşünürsün, konuşma akmaya devam eder.

Üç altın kural

  1. Düşünce ve his fiillerini sade kullan: I prefer… · I completely agree… · I understand why… · I know… · I need…
  2. Bugünkü alışkanlık = present simple, eski alışkanlık = used to, olay = past simple. I work from home. In Istanbul, I used to spend two hours in traffic. Last year, I took up sewing.
  3. Zaman = take. It takes me ten minutes to get to work. · …which would normally take six months.

Interview'daki 4 soru: Hangisine hangi açılış?

Interview hep aynı sırayla ilerler: senden başlar, topluma doğru genişler. Soru numarasını duyunca açılışın hazır olsun.

Soru Ne sorar Açılış kalıbı Örnek kullan
1. Kişisel Sen ne yapıyorsun, nasıl? Personally, I… / In my case, I usually… Bugünkü alışkanlık (present simple)
2. Tercih / değişim Hangisini tercih edersin? I like both, but I mostly prefer… / When I first started…, but now I… Eskiden–şimdi (used to → now)
3. Görüş / trend Bu trend hakkında ne düşünüyorsun? I think this trend makes sense, mainly because… / It depends, but in most cases… Bir olay + This shows that…
4. Toplum / gelecek Herkes için iyi mi? Gelecekte ne olur? In the long run, I believe… / If more people…, society would… Kendi deneyimin + genelleme

Dört soruda da kapanış aynı: So, overall, …

Konu haritası: Her alan için bir hazır örnek (Speaking + Writing)

TOEFL soruları hep aynı alanlardan gelir. Her alan için bir örneğin hazır olsun, soru gelince tablo aklına gelsin. Sınav dili ölçer, hikâyenin doğruluğunu değil; bu yüzden hepsini rahatça kullanabilirsin. Her birini bir kez sesli söyle ki sınavda tanıdık gelsin.

Alan Örnek Kalıplı mini cevap
Şehir & yaşam İstanbul → Urla In Istanbul, I used to spend two hours on the road every day. Now I live in Urla, so instead of sitting in traffic, I spend time with my family.
İş & verimlilik Hibrit çalışma *The best thing about hybrid work is that I save two hours a day. That said, I still go to the office twice a week to see my team.*
Teknoloji & AI AI kodlama araçları *For example, my team recently built a system in one week, which would normally take six months. This shows that AI can save huge amounts of time.*
Eğitim & öğrenme Lisede İngilizce I learned English in high school, but it wasn't until I started working that I really used it. This made me realize that practice matters more than grammar rules.
Hobi & stres Dikiş *I started sewing last year, and creating something with my own hands makes me much less stressed.*
Medya & ekran süresi Ekransız hafta *Last year, I tried a screen-free week. It made me realize how much time I was wasting on my phone.*
Doğa & spor Belgrad Ormanı *Whenever I feel tired after work, I go for a walk by the sea. It helps me clear my head and sleep better.*
Sağlık & beslenme Evde yemek *Since I moved to Urla, I cook at home almost every day, and I feel much healthierthan before.*
Çevre İkinci el kumaş *Instead of buying new fabric, I now buy second-hand fabric. Not only is it cheaper, but it also cuts my waste.*
Para & alışveriş Harcama takibi *Last year, I started tracking my spending in an app. As a result, I saved enough for a holiday.*
Para & deneyim ABD'de 40 gün *In my opinion, young people should spend money on experiences. For example, I recently stayed in the United States for 40 days, and it was worth every penny.*
Aile & ilişkiler Pazar aramaları *Since I moved, I call my family every Sunday. Being far away has taught me to value that time much more.*
Seyahat & kültür Ege köyü *One of the best experiences I've had was visiting a small Aegean village. I learnedhow locals make olive oil, which I never would have learned from a book.*
Topluluk & gönüllülük Sahil temizliği *If more people joined local projects, our towns would be much cleaner. **For example,**last year I joined a beach clean-up with twenty neighbors.*
Takım çalışması İş projesi *In my experience, clear roles are the key to teamwork. For example, I once led a project, and because we shared tasks clearly, we finished two weeks early.*
Seyahat & tercih Roma/Floransa → ABD milli parkları *When I first started traveling abroad, I mostly preferred famous cities, like Rome. Now I prefer quieter places, like national parks in the United States.*

Bir hikâye, birden fazla soru: Soru tam olarak tabloya uymuyorsa en yakın alanı seç. "Gençler çok mu telefon kullanıyor?" → Medya. "Şirketler çalışanlara ne sunmalı?" → İş. "Turizm yerel halka faydalı mı?" → Seyahat.

Örnek tam cevap (seyahat): Famous tourist cities or small, quiet places? "I like both, but my preference has changed over time. When I first started traveling abroad, I mostly preferred famous cities. For example, I went to Rome and Florence with my family, and we loved the museums and restaurants. However, those places were really crowded. Now I prefer quieter places, like national parks in the United States, because I can relax in nature. So, overall, I think visiting both kinds of places is the best option."

Örnek tam cevap (para): Save money for the future or spend it on experiences now? "My answer has changed over time. When I first started making money, I mostly preferred saving it for the future. Nowadays, though, I preferspending it on new experiences. For example, I recently went to the United States and stayed there for 40 days. It cost me a lot of money, but I experienced so many different things. In my opinion, young people should try new things while they can. So, overall, the key is to balance saving money and enjoying life."

Band 6 kelimeler (birini her cevaba koy)

recharge · switch off · pick up a skill · in detail · now and then · instead of + -ing · as long as · suit my lifestyle · work-life balance · much less stressful · far more convenient

Listen and Repeat

Cümleyi anlam grupları halinde dinle: Group classes start | at seven a.m. | and you can sign up | online. Küçük kelimeleri de söyle: a / the · -s · -ed · at / on / in. Önce yapıyı (özne + fiil) yakala, sonra hatırladığın ayrıntıları ekle.

2. Write an Email: 7 Dakika, ~120 Kelime

Bu senin en güçlü bölümün (4/5). Bildiğin yöntemi aynen ve hızlı uygula.

Zaman planı

Süre Ne yapıyorsun
0:00–3:30 Her maddeyi "I" ile yeniden yaz, madde başına 2 cümle (~90 kelime). Maddedeki fiili cümlene taşı (explain → I'm writing to explain…, suggest → I'd like to suggest…).
3:30–5:30 Her paragrafa bir cümle ekle: sonuç (As a result…), alternatif (Alternatively…) ya da sayı/tarih.
5:30–7:00 Kontrol: Her madde var mı? "And" ile iki parçalı madde varsa iki parçası da var mı? Sayılar görevle aynı mı? could kelimesini kontrol et.

İskelet: Paragraf paragraf (~120 kelime)

Açılış (1 cümle, ~15 kelime): Amacı söyle.

  • I am writing about the cooking class that was scheduled for last Saturday.
  • I am writing to let you know about a problem with my recent order.
  • I hope you're doing well! I'm writing because I have some exciting news. (arkadaş)

Paragraf 1 (~35 kelime): Durum: ne oldu, ne zaman, ne kadar?

  • On [tarih], I [ne yaptın]. However, [ne ters gitti].
  • I registered three weeks ago and paid $60. However, I received an email only one day before the class saying it had been cancelled.
  • Sayı ve tarih ekle: three weeks ago · $60 · about twenty people · next Friday at 7 p.m.

Paragraf 2 (~35 kelime): Etki / ayrıntı: bu sana ne yaptı?

  • The late cancellation =position. (olay özne)
  • This position. As a result, I wasted two hours, and I had already turned down my friends’ plans.
  • I had already [hazırlık], and I had [vazgeçtiğin şey].
  • As a result, I lost both [para] and [zaman/plan].

Paragraf 3 (~35 kelime): Çözüm / istek: ne istiyorsun?

  • It might help to [öneri 1].
  • Alternatively, I would appreciate it if you could [öneri 2].
  • Should this not be possible, [yedek öneri] would also be acceptable.
  • İkna gerekiyorsa: Not only would this [fayda 1], but it would also [fayda 2].

Kapanış (1 cümle)

  • Resmi: Thank you for your understanding. / I look forward to hearing from you. + Best regards, Sena Oz
  • Arkadaş: Let me know what you think! / Can't wait to see you! + Best, Sena

Kontrol cümlesi: Görevdeki her madde = bir paragraf. Her paragrafta bir ayrıntı (sayı/tarih) + bir sonuç cümlesi var mı?

Diplomatik dil: Olayı özne yap: The late cancellation left us… · The delay meant that… İkna için: Not only would this…, but it would also… · In return, we could… at no cost to the city.

İmza: Sena Oz

Cheat sheet: Madde böyle sorulursa, böyle yaz

Görevdeki her madde bu fiillerden biriyle gelir. Fiili gör, sağdaki iki cümleyi doldur.

Madde şöyle derse Böyle yaz
explain what happened / describe the problem I am writing about the cooking class on Saturday. On Friday evening, I received an email saying it had been cancelled, but I had already paid the full fee.
describe how it affected you This left me in a difficult position. As a result, I wasted two hours, and I had alreadyturned down my friends' plans.
suggest a solution / make a suggestion It might help to offer a replacement class next month. Alternatively, you could give us a discount on a future course.
ask for something / make a request I would appreciate it if you could refund my fee by the end of the week. Should this not be possible, a voucher would also be acceptable.
ask for information Could you let me know whether the course will still start on Monday?
apologize I am really sorry for returning your book late. Unfortunately, I was ill last week, so I could not come to campus.
thank someone Thank you so much for helping me with the presentation. It really helped me feel more confident in the meeting.
decline / say you can't attend I'm afraid I won't be able to come to your birthday dinner, because I have a work trip that weekend.
accept / say you will attend I would be delighted to join the farewell party. I can bring a cake and some drinks.
give details (time/place/plan) The party will take place on Friday at 7 p.m. in the office kitchen. About 20 people will join.
offer help / say what you can do I would be happy to organize the clean-up. In return, we could keep the garden tidy at no cost to the city.
explain why your idea is good / persuade Not only would this save money, but it would also make the area safer for children.
recommend / give advice I would recommend visiting Urla, because it is quiet and the food is excellent. For example, last summer I tried a lovely seafood restaurant by the harbor.

Kime yazdığına göre açılış ve kapanış

Kime Açılış Kapanış
Yönetici, kurum, belediye, hoca Dear Ms. Clark, / Dear Professor Lee,+ I am writing about… Thank you for your understanding. / I look forward to hearing from you. + Best regards,
Arkadaş, iş arkadaşı Hi Emma, + I hope you're doing well! Can't wait to see you! / Let me know what you think. + Best, / Cheers,

Arkadaşa yazarken de tam cümle ve her madde şart. Sadece ton rahat: I'm, can't, Let's…

Favori yapın: Not only… but also…

Bu yapı tek başına Variety and word choice puanını yükseltir. Her yazıda bir kez kullan, en etkili yerde.

1. Cümle ortasında (kolay): The restaurant was not only slow but also expensive. AI tools not only save time but alsoreduce mistakes.

2. Cümle başında (daha etkileyici, soru sırasıyla): *Not only was the food cold, but the service was also slow.* *Not only would this save money, but it would also help the environment.* *Not only does hybrid work save time, but it also improves my focus.*

Kurmak için cümlenin soru halini düşün:

Düz cümle Soru hali Not only ile
The food was cold. Was the food cold? *Not only was the food cold, but…*
It saves time. Does it save time? *Not only does it save time, but it also…*
This would help. Would this help? *Not only would this help, but it would also…*

Hangi bölümde nerede kullanılır:

  • Email (öneri paragrafı): Not only would this make lunch easier, but it would also give the team more time to talk.
  • Academic Discussion (yeni fikir): Not only does remote work reduce traffic, but it also gives people more time with their families.
  • Speaking (sebep): Not only is it cheaper, but it's also much more convenient.

"On behalf of ___", "… adına" demek. Bir grup adına teşekkür ederken kullanılır, yani sadece kendin için değil, ekibin, kulübün ya da komşuların adına konuşuyorsun. Özellikle resmi teşekkür e-postalarının ilk cümlesi için çok uygundur.

Kalıp: On behalf of [grup], I would like to thank you for [isim / fiil-ing].

Birinci boşluk (kimin adına):

  • On behalf of our team…
  • On behalf of everyone at Bright Wave Tech…

İkinci boşluk (neden teşekkür): for'dan sonra ya isim ya da fiil + -ing gelir.

  • …thank you for the excellent workshop last Friday.

  • …thank you for taking the time to speak at our event.

  • …thank you for organizing such a useful training session.

  • Konuk konuşmacı: On behalf of the student organization, I would like to thank you for taking the time to speak at our event last Thursday.

  • Ms. Tanaka'nın workshop'u: On behalf of our team, I would like to thank you for organizing such a practical workshop.

  • Belediyeye: On behalf of all the residents of our building, I would like to thank you for repairing the street lights so quickly.

Elaboration -> Sonraki cümlede teşekkürü somut bir ayrıntıyla destekle: "Your tips on time management have already helped us finish our weekly reports faster."

SAMPLE: Kevin

Hi Kevin,

Thank you again for recommending the new Italian restaurant downtown. I took the team there for lunch last Thursday, but unfortunately, the visit did not live up to our expectations. The pizza arrived lukewarm, and the portions were surprisingly small for the price. On top of that, the restaurant seemed understaffed, so we waited almost 40 minutes for our meals.

As a result, the team was a bit disappointed. A few colleagues had to leave before dessert to make it to a 2 p.m. meeting, and others joked that we should have brought sandwiches instead.

For our next team lunch, we could go back to the seafood restaurant near the harbor. Not only is the food consistently good there, but the staff are also used to serving large groups. Alternatively, we could order in and eat together in the meeting room. Let me know what you think!

Best, Sena

3. Academic Discussion: 10 Dakika, 120–140 Kelime

Bu iskelet sana üç şeyi garanti ediyor: ilk cümlede net görüş, tamamlanmış bir metin ve fikre bağlanan bir örnek.

Zaman planı

Süre Ne yapıyorsun
0:00–1:00 Soruyu oku. Evet mi hayır mı? Karar ver. Hangi öğrenciye cevap vereceğini ve hikâye bankasından hangi örneği kullanacağını seç.
1:00–5:00 İskeleti doldur (~100 kelime).
5:00–8:30 Örneği genişlet, "This shows that" cümlesini güçlendir.
8:30–10:00 Kontrol. 9:00'da ne yazıyorsan bitir, sonuç cümlesi mutlaka olsun.

İskelet: Blok blok (120–140 kelime)

Blok 1: Net görüş (1 cümle, ~20 kelime): Soruya ilk cümlede evet/hayır.

  • I strongly believe that governments should encourage people to move to small towns.
  • In my view, online learning is far more effective than traditional classes for working adults.

Blok 2: Öğrenciye cevap (2 cümle, ~30 kelime): Adıyla an, söylediğini kısaca tekrar et, sonra karşılık ver.

  • I see Daniel's point that most jobs are in big cities, but this is changing quickly.
  • Thanks to [gelişme], [neden onun fikri eksik].
  • Katılıyorsan: I agree with Maya that…, and I would add that…

Blok 3: Yeni fikir (1–2 cümle, ~25 kelime): İki öğrencinin söylemediği bir neden.

  • Another important reason is that [yeni fikir].
  • What is often overlooked is that [yeni fikir].
  • In the long run, [uzun vadeli etki].

Blok 4: Örnek + bağlantı (2–3 cümle, ~40 kelime): Konu haritasından al, geçmiş zamanla anlat, görüşe bağla.

  • For example, when I [olay], I [sonuç]. This shows that [görüşün doğru çünkü].
  • For example, when I moved from Istanbul to a small town, I kept the same job because I could work online. This shows that people can keep their careers without living in crowded cities.

Blok 5: Sonuç (1 cümle, ~15 kelime): Görüşü farklı kelimelerle tekrar et.

  • That is why I believe... [görüş].
  • For these reasons, I firmly believe that... [görüş].

Kelime kontrolü: 20 + 30 + 25 + 40 + 15 ≈ 130 kelime. 5:00'te Blok 1–3 bitmiş olsun, Blok 4'ü sonra genişlet.

Soru tipi → ilk cümle ve öğrenciye cevap

Profesörün sorusu hep bu dört tipten biridir. Tipi gör, ilk iki cümlen hazır.

Soru tipi İlk cümle (net görüş) Öğrenciye cevap
Agree / disagree (Do you agree that…?) I strongly agree that… / I disagree with the idea that… I see Daniel's point that…, but…
Which is better (A or B?) In my view, A is far more effective than B, mainly because… Maya makes a good point about…, but she overlooks…
Should the government / schools / companies…? I believe governments should…, because… While I understand Daniel's concern about…, I think…
Advantages / most important factor In my opinion, the most important factor is… I agree with Maya that…, and I would add that…

Yeni fikir için açılışlar (her seferinde birini seç): Another important reason is that… · What is often overlooked is that… · In the long run, … · If more people…, society would…

Örnek + bağlantı: Konu haritasından ilgili satırı al, geçmiş zamanla yaz, sonra This shows that… ile görüşüne bağla.

Soru: Should companies allow remote work? → Harita: İş & verimlilik

For example, when my company introduced hybrid work, I saved two hours a day and finished my tasks faster. This shows that remote work can make employees more productive.

Kapanış: That is why I believe… · For these reasons, I am convinced that…

Örnek seçimi

Yukarıdaki konu haritası burada da geçerli. Discussion konusu hangi alandansa o satırdaki örneği al ve geçmiş zamanla bir-iki cümleye genişlet.

Örneğin şehirleşme → Urla, çevre politikası → sahil temizliği, okul müfredatı → lisede İngilizce, vergi/ekonomi → harcama takibi.

Kişisel örnekler, yaklaşık sayılar ve mantıklı ayrıntılar metni güçlendirir; rahatça kullan.

4. Build a Sentence: Tek Kural

Do you know / Can you tell me / I wonder sonrası düz cümle sırası:

Do you know when the library closes? · Can you tell me where the room is? · I wonder when the results will be announced.

Aynı fiilin iki hali varsa cümleye uyanı seç. Emin olduğun parçalarla başla, kalanı en sona yerleştir.

  • [ ] Beş durak: cevap → The main reason is that → For example → That said → So, overall

  • [ ] Email: her madde var mı, could doğru mu, Sena Oz
